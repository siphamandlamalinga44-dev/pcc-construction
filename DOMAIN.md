# Domain & Email Setup — PC Chauke Construction and Projects CC

Your site is live at **https://pcc-construction.onrender.com**. A custom `.co.za`
domain makes it official and professional.

## 1. Pick a domain (all checked and AVAILABLE on 2026-11-17)

| Domain | Best for |
|---|---|
| `pcchaukeconstruction.co.za` | Full company name — recommended |
| `pcchauke.co.za` | Short, memorable, easy to say |
| `pcchaukeprojects.co.za` | Matches "Construction and Projects" |
| `pcchaukebuild.co.za` | Short alternative |

## 2. Register it

Cheapest verified registrars (.co.za):

- **Truehost** — around R45–R89 promo for the first year (cheapest), then renewals
- **Register.co.za** — around R99/year
- **Afrihost** — R197 first year, R99 renewal

At checkout you'll need:
- The domain name you chose
- Your name, email, physical address and ID number (South African residents)
- Payment by card or EFT

The domain activates within a few hours (some registrars up to 24–48h).

## 3. Point the domain to Render

1. In the Render dashboard open the **pcc-construction** service
   (https://dashboard.render.com/web/srv-dbu463psrm7s73eihssg).
2. Go to **Domains → Add domain** and enter e.g. `pcchaukeconstruction.co.za`.
3. Render shows the DNS records to add at your registrar:
   - `A` record → Render's IP (provided in the dashboard)
   - Or `CNAME` → the Render hostname
4. In your registrar's DNS/management panel, add those records for the domain.
5. Wait a few minutes, then open your domain in a browser — it should load the site.

> Tip: keep the `pcc-construction.onrender.com` URL as a fallback while DNS propagates.

## 4. Business email (optional, after the domain)

Once the domain is active you can create email addresses (e.g. `info@pcchaukeconstruction.co.za`):

- **Google Workspace** (Gmail for business) — easy setup, ~R75/user/month
- **Microsoft 365** — includes email + Office
- Most registrars also offer basic mail boxes (cheaper)

Then add your email details in **Admin → Email Settings** so the contact form
emails you directly, and update the site's email address in **Admin → Website Text**.

## 5. Google Business Profile (free, do it now)

1. Go to **business.google.com** and click *Manage now*.
2. Add your business name, category (*Construction contractor*), address/service
   area (all provinces), phone and website.
3. Verify by postcard or video (Google sends a code).

This makes you appear in Google Maps and "near me" searches — the single best
free marketing step for a construction company.
